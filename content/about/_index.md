Hi! I'm Effy, you can also call me 李雪 [lǐ xuě]. I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at CWI Amsterdam, in the [TRL lab](https://trl-lab.github.io/), led by [dr. ir. Madelon Hulsebos](https://www.madelonhulsebos.com/). I am interested in the topic of evaluating and building ==Agentic Data Science==.

I did my PhD at University of Amsterdam in [INDE lab](https://indelab.org/), supervised by [Prof. Paul Groth](https://pgroth.com) and [Dr. Jan-Christoph Kalo](https://scholar.google.de/citations?user=2EE-YUsAAAAJ&hl=de). During my PhD, I did an internship at [MotherDuck](https://motherduck.com/) on data wrangling with LLMs.
Previously I was an AI resident at Microsoft Research Cambridge, UK. Before that I did my Master's at University of Edinburgh.

My PhD topic was on ==Knowledge Graph Construction from Conversational Data==. Specifically, I look into how we can better utilize (large) language models to extract information such as entities and relations, and furthermore, make (large) language models more data-efficient, robust and adaptable. My PhD research was funded and situated in an NWO project [*IN-SIGHT.it*](https://in-sight.it/).

I am originally from China and have lived in the UK and the Netherlands. I truly value helping and supporting each other, and so far I have guided 40+ students through their Master's and PhD applications. When I am not in front of my laptop, you can often find me bouldering (at a complete beginner level), cycling or drinking coffee (like everyone else :D). If you are keen to collaborate, would like support with your application, or just want to exchange ideas over a coffee, you can reach out to me at `effy DOT li AT cwi DOT nl`.
